# Compounds considered

PROBAND01. Not medical care, a diagnosis, or advice. Nothing here is a
recommendation to start, stop, or change a medicine.

Expansion of the reject / research table. **Not a candidate list.**
The candidate table in `bartholomewtj_track2_report.md` stays empty.
Conditional on biallelic *BUB1B*. Missense unproven and unphased.

Valid-drug test: raise checkpoint in patient cells; do not worsen
mitotic fidelity; do not trade growth.

| Compound / class | Verdict | Key DOI | Why / why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| BubR1 replacement (cDNA / gene addition) | Research only. Only proven restore. Not a medicine. | [10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319) Suijkerbuijk 2010; [10.1038/ncb2643](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb2643) Baker 2013; [10.1038/ng1449](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1449) Hanks 2004 | Patient-cell nocodazole arrest restored by putting BubR1 back. Extra BubR1 in WT mice cuts aneuploidy and cancer. No approved product. |
| Extra MAD2 or BUB1 | Reject | [10.1038/ncb2643](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb2643) Baker 2013 | Extra BubR1 protects; extra Mad2 or Bub1 is oncogenic in mice. “More SAC protein” is not a general rule. |
| SIRT2 overexpression (genetic) | Research only. Not a drug. | [10.15252/embj.201386907](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.201386907) North 2014 | SIRT2tg on BubR1H/H: +58% median lifespan, males only. Mice stay small. Cataracts/kyphosis stay. Partial heart rescue. |
| NMN | Research only. Not a medicine. Not something to start. | [10.15252/embj.201386907](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.201386907) North 2014 | Only published chemical that raised BubR1. Aged WT mouse testes, 500 mg/kg/day IP × 7; SIRT2-dependent in MEFs. Not hypomorph. Not patient cells. |
| Nicotinamide riboside (NR) | Research only. Not a medicine. Not something to start. | No BubR1 paper in hand | Same polarity guess as NMN. Supplement. Orphan designation MELAS, not approved. Restore untested. |
| Niacin (nicotinic acid) | Not a candidate. Not something to start. | No BubR1 paper. Mechanism: North 2014 above | Only approved NAD-ish drug. Preiss–Handler route, not NAM. Could raise NAD, do nothing, or harm (flush/liver/glucose). I am not using a stretch of “approved” to fill the empty table. |
| Nicotinamide (NAM) | Reject. Wrong direction. | [10.15252/embj.201386907](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.201386907) North 2014; [10.1098/rstb.2017.0070](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2017.0070) Kudo 2018 | Sirtuin product inhibitor. Drops BubR1. Kudo used 20 mM NAM as the positive control that lowers protein. Not interchangeable with NMN. |
| SIRT2 inhibitors (SirReal2, NPD11033, sirtinol) | Reject. Wrong direction. | [10.1038/ncomms7263](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7263) Rumpf 2015; [10.1098/rstb.2017.0070](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2017.0070) Kudo 2018 | Destabilise BubR1. Chemical tools on this axis are inhibitors. No approved SIRT2 activator exists. |
| HDAC inhibitors (vorinostat-class) | Reject as a SIRT2 stand-in | North 2014 (K668 acetylation) | Zinc HDACs, not SIRT2. Raising acetylation would be the destruction mark on K668. Do not confuse with a SIRT2 activator. |
| CBP/p300 inhibitors (inobrodib / CCS1477) | Research tool. Not a candidate. | Mechanism: North 2014. No MVA paper. Clinical: NCT04068597 | Chemical inverse of K668 acetylation. CBP is a general transcription coactivator (MYC, AR, HIF). Oncology schedule, thrombocytopenia. Making CIN worse via transcription is plausible. |
| Pharmacological chaperone (generic / HSP90-helper) | No molecule. Theoretical pass if abundance hypomorph. | [10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319) Suijkerbuijk 2010; allelic caveat [10.1172/jci126863](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci126863) Sieben 2020 | Kinase-domain missenses are mostly 2–6× abundance hypomorphs. Overexpressing them to WT level restores arrest. No molecule that raises mutant BubR1. Dies if the missense is Y155C-class (stable, checkpoint-dead). Neighbourhood ≠ assay. |
| Sodium phenylbutyrate / 4-PBA | No evidence. Dual mechanism may cancel. | No BubR1 paper. Chaperone idea: Suijkerbuijk 2010 | Approved (urea cycle, paediatric). Chemical chaperone in other misfolding papers. Also an HDAC inhibitor → more acetylation → possible faster K668 turnover. Untested. Most 4-PBA chaperone trials in other diseases were poor. |
| HSP90 inhibitors (geldanamycin, 17-AAG, tanespimycin, ganetespib) | Reject. Wrong direction. | [10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319) Suijkerbuijk Fig. 3C | HSP90 already folds kinase-domain missense BubR1. Inhibitors dump it. None approved. |
| APC/C–CDC20 inhibitors (apcin, proTAME) | Research only. Constitutive hold. | [10.1038/nature13660](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13660) Sackton 2014; [10.1038/s41589-020-0495-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-020-0495-z) Richeson 2020 | Chemical MCC: hold APC/C without more BubR1. Oncology “block mitotic exit” playbook — freezes every cell, fails growth. Apcin binds the Cdc20 site used by MCC; prolongs mitosis when SAC is low, shortens it when SAC is high. Residual MCC in MVA1 could be stolen. No clinical candidate. |
| Proteasome inhibitors (bortezomib-class) | Reject as a restore | North 2014 (K668 → ubiquitin → proteasome) | Might spare BubR1 from degradation and freeze cyclin B destruction. That is a mitotic poison, not a conditional SAC. Neuropathy, infection risk. |
| TRIP13 inhibitors (DCZ0415, TI17) | Reject. Opposite polarity. | [10.1038/s41467-018-06774-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06774-1) Eytan 2018; DCZ0415 abstract [10.1158/0008-5472.can-18-3987](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.can-18-3987) Wang 2020 (abstract only) | TRIP13 turns the SAC off and keeps a pool of open MAD2 so it can turn on. Lasting loss depletes O-Mad2; checkpoint fails. BubR1-low MCC is already weak. Same logic for p31comet (*MAD2L1BP*) blockade. |
| Aurora B inhibitors (barasertib, tozasertib, …) | Reject | Open Targets screen; SAC logic in Suijkerbuijk 2010 | Weaken error-correction / checkpoint. None approved. MVA1 already has a weak checkpoint. |
| PLK1 inhibitors (volasertib, onvansertib) | Reject | Open Targets screen | Same. None approved. Volasertib reached phase 3 in other diseases. |
| MPS1/TTK inhibitors (BAY-1217389, …) | Reject | Open Targets screen | SAC kinase inhibitors. Phase 1. Wrong direction. |
| Microtubule poisons (vincristine, paclitaxel, vinblastine, colchicine, eribulin, vedotin ADCs) | Reject as MVA treatment. Avoid if a tumour is treated. | [10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.can-09-4319) Suijkerbuijk; [10.1086/303022](https://doi.org/10.1086/303022) Matsuura 2000; [10.1038/32688](https://doi.org/10.1038/32688) Cahill 1998 | Need a working SAC to hold the arrest. MVA1 cells fail nocodazole/STLC/colcemid arrest. Approved cytotoxics on a cancer-predisposition background. |
| Rapalogs (sirolimus, everolimus, ridaforolimus) | Trap. Not SAC restore. | [10.1172/jci126863](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci126863) Sieben 2020 | Monoallelic BubR1 mouse sarcopenia linked to mTORC1 hyperactivity; they cited aging-rapamycin papers; they did not treat those mice. Rapalogs impair growth. MVA already includes growth restriction. Used in children with TSC — different disease. |
| Senolytics / p16+ cell clearance | Adjacent. Not restore. | [10.1038/nature10600](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10600) Baker 2011; [10.1038/ng1382](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1382) Baker 2004 | In BubR1H/H mice, killing p16+ cells delayed cataracts/sarcopenia/fat loss. Did not extend lifespan (heart). H/H is an aging model, few tumours. Not childhood MVA1. |
| HSCT | Adjacent, wrong tissue. One case. | [10.1186/s13023-019-1073-x](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13023-019-1073-x) Laberko 2019 | 11-year-old MVA1 with MDS/−7. Engrafted, rejected day +47, died day +156 (*E. faecium*). Not whole-body SAC restore. Different question if MDS/−7 appears later. Do not generalise. |
| Aneuploid-cell killing (AICAR, chloroquine, 17-AAG) | Reject as MVA treatment. Oncology CIN playbook. | [10.1016/j.cell.2011.01.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2011.01.017) Tang 2011, abstract only | Named as trisomy-selective. Restore fail. 17-AAG is geldanamycin-class (dumps mutant BubR1). Chloroquine approved antimalarial — using it to kill mosaic aneuploid cells in a child is not a restore. Metformin is an AMPK-ish stand-in for AICAR; same problem: killing aneuploid cells is not restoring the checkpoint. |
| UMK57 (MCAK / kinesin-13 activator) | Adjacent. Alignment, not SAC. | [10.1016/j.celrep.2016.10.030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2016.10.030) Orr 2016; [10.15252/embr.201949248](https://doi.org/10.15252/embr.201949248) Barroso-Vilares 2020 | May help BubR1’s attachment job, not the checkpoint. Cancer cells adapt via Aurora B in days. Aged fibroblasts: less mis-segregation, not MVA. Research only. |
| Sirt2–BubR1-K243 (oocytes) | Analogical only. Wrong tissue. | [10.1111/acel.12698](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12698) Qiu 2018 | Same polarity as North, different lysine, meiosis not soma. Not a somatic SAC restore. |
| Approved SIRT2 activator | Empty. None exist. | Open Targets *SIRT2* = 0 clinical | Tools on this target are inhibitors (see SirReal2 / NPD11033). SIRT6 (forvisirvat) and SIRT1 (resveratrol-class) are the wrong isoform. |
